# Traceability

Joins canonical use cases, requirements, architecture, design, source, checks and results without duplicating definitions.
There are 30 committed and 16 active draft requirements, eight current and eight proposed live architecture elements, three current and three proposed use cases, plus four superseded ARCH IDs, two superseded draft ARCH IDs, eleven superseded draft requirement IDs, and one superseded draft use-case ID.
[Conventions](./conventions.md) define status.
Allocation identifies responsibility; the [qualification assessment](verification/assessment.md) records demonstrated requirement verdicts and remaining evidence limits.

## Committed requirement allocation

UC-01 covers archive inspection; UC-02 covers bounded exploration.
CLI help/verbose support the inspection workflow; REQ-LIB-01 originates in distribution intent rather than a separate minted UC.
Row IDs and obligations link both ways; architecture/UC tables provide reverse joins.
Verification assertions are specified once in the [committed requirement measure map](swe.6-software-verification/01-requirement-verification.md), with [unit](swe.4-unit-verification/01-verification-measures.md) and [component/integration](swe.5-component-and-integration-verification/index.md) detail.
[Shared results](./verification/index.md) distinguish historical runs, inspected measures and fresh checks.
[The real-case role acceptance matrix](../verification/results/real-case-user-validation.md#measures) joins AI-agent, engineer and CI checks to affected REQ IDs; its executed results supplement the per-requirement qualification assessment.
[The agent-led AOSP investigation](../verification/results/agent-led-aosp-crash-investigation.md#swe-process-and-traceability) demonstrates adaptive MCP evidence use under REQ-MCP-03/06/09/12 and explicitly separates external platform candidate obligations from this product's contracts.
Source links locate implementation without proving obligations.
