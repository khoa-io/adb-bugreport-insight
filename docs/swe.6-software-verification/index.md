# Software verification

SWE.6 checks integrated behavior against the [committed requirements](../swe.1-requirements/02-software-requirements.md).
The [measure map](01-requirement-verification.md) defines checks and coverage boundaries.
[Traceability](../traceability.md) joins obligations to architecture, detailed design and implementation.

The [assessment](../verification/assessment.md) records 30 requirement verdicts and the current platform limit.
Completing an assessment does not turn an unverified clause into a pass; deferred proposals stay outside the committed set.
[Insight generation](../../verification/results/insight-generation.md) and [resource evidence](../../verification/results/mcp-insight-resources.md) support their applicable requirements independently of [open qualification](../verification/03-open-qualification.md).
